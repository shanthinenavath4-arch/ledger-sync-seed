## Referral Feedback

I referred the Simplify Money app to three people and collected feedback from them.

### Person 1

**What they liked**

- The app provides a simple way to view and track financial activity.
- The overall experience was easy to understand.

**What they did not like**

- Some parts of the app could provide more clarity about how transactions are categorized.
- The initial experience could be made more intuitive for a new user.

### Person 2

**What they liked**

- The interface was straightforward to explore.
- The transaction-tracking concept was useful.

**What they did not like**

- More detailed information about individual transactions would be helpful.
- The app could make important actions and information more noticeable.

### Person 3

**What they liked**

- The app focuses on bringing financial activity into one place.
- The overall concept was easy to understand.

**What they did not like**

- Transaction information could be presented with more context.
- The app could provide clearer explanations when a transaction is identified or categorized.

## Common Feedback

### Positive

- Simple and easy-to-understand financial tracking concept.
- Useful to have transaction activity available in one place.
- The interface was generally straightforward to explore.

### What users did not like

- Transaction categorization could be clearer.
- More context around individual transactions would be useful.
- The first-time user experience could provide more guidance.

## My Observation

The most useful feedback was around:

- Making transaction categorization more transparent.
- Providing more context for individual transactions.
- Improving the first-time user experience and guidance.

I would use this feedback when evaluating the product experience and deciding which parts of the transaction-tracking flow need the most attention.
